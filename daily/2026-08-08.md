6. **[Medium] Probability Distribution**
   - EN: A fair die is rolled. What is the expected value (mean) of the result?
   - FR: On lance un dé équitable. Quelle est la valeur attendue (moyenne) du résultat?
   - Choices: A) 2.5  B) 3  C) 3.5  D) 4
   - Hint: Expected value = sum of (each value × its probability) (e.g., a coin flip: E = 0 × 0.5 + 1 × 0.5 = 0.5).
   - Steps:
     - E = (1+2+3+4+5+6) × (1/6) = 21/6 = 3.5.
     - The expected value is 3.5.
   - Answer: 3.5

7. **[Medium] Vector Addition**
   - EN: Vector **a** = (3, 4) and vector **b** = (−1, 6). What is **a** + **b**?
   - FR: Le vecteur **a** = (3, 4) et le vecteur **b** = (−1, 6). Quel est **a** + **b**?
   - Choices: A) (1, 9)  B) (2, 10)  C) (3, 11)  D) (4, 2)
   - Hint: Add vectors component by component (e.g., (2,5) + (3,−1) = (2+3, 5+(−1)) = (5, 4)).
   - Steps:
     - x-components: 3 + (−1) = 2; y-components: 4 + 6 = 10.
     - **a** + **b** = (2, 10).
   - Answer: (2, 10)

8. **[Medium] Derivative — Rate of Change**
   - EN: A function gives the position of a ball as s(t) = 3t² − 6t + 1, where t is in seconds and s is in metres. What is the velocity of the ball at t = 4 seconds?
   - FR: Une fonction donne la position d'une balle : s(t) = 3t² − 6t + 1, où t est en secondes et s en mètres. Quelle est la vitesse de la balle à t = 4 secondes?
   - Choices: A) 12 m/s  B) 18 m/s  C) 24 m/s  D) 30 m/s
   - Hint: Velocity = derivative of position; differentiate s(t), then substitute the given time (e.g., s(t) = 2t² + 3: s'(t) = 4t; at t=3, velocity = 12 m/s).
   - Steps:
     - s'(t) = 6t − 6.
     - At t = 4: s'(4) = 6(4) − 6 = 24 − 6 = 18 m/s.
   - Answer: 18 m/s

9. **[Hard] Binomial Theorem**
   - EN: What is the coefficient of x² in the expansion of (x + 3)⁴?
   - FR: Quel est le coefficient de x² dans le développement de (x + 3)⁴?
   - Choices: A) 27  B) 54  C) 81  D) 108
   - Hint: Use the binomial theorem: the term with xʳ has coefficient C(n, r) × aⁿ⁻ʳ × bʳ (e.g., in (x+2)³, coefficient of x¹ = C(3,1)×2² = 3×4 = 12).
   - Steps:
     - The x² term: C(4,2) × x² × 3² = 6 × 9 × x² = 54x².
     - The coefficient is 54.
   - Answer: 54

10. **[Hard] Conditional Probability**
    - EN: In a group of 100 students, 60 play hockey and 40 do not. Of those who play hockey, 45 also volunteer. Of those who do not play hockey, 15 volunteer. Given that a student volunteers, what is the probability that they play hockey?
    - FR: Dans un groupe de 100 élèves, 60 jouent au hockey et 40 ne jouent pas. Parmi ceux qui jouent au hockey, 45 font aussi du bénévolat. Parmi ceux qui ne jouent pas, 15 font du bénévolat. Sachant qu'un élève fait du bénévolat, quelle est la probabilité qu'il joue au hockey?
    - Choices: A) 0.45  B) 0.60  C) 0.75  D) 0.90
    - Hint: Use Bayes' theorem: P(hockey|volunteer) = P(volunteer and hockey) ÷ P(volunteer) (e.g., if 30 out of 50 volunteers play sport A, probability = 30/50 = 0.6).
    - Steps:
      - Volunteers who play hockey: 45; total volunteers: 45 + 15 = 60.
      - P(hockey | volunteer) = 45/60 = 0.75.
    - Answer: 0.75
