    • Hint: Use C(n, r) = n! ÷ (r! × (n−r)!) — like C(4, 2) = 4! ÷ (2! × 2!) = 24 ÷ 4 = 6.
    • Steps:
      • C(5, 2) = 5! ÷ (2! × 3!) = (5 × 4) ÷ (2 × 1).
      • = 20 ÷ 2 = 10.
      • There are 10 ways.
    • Answer: 10

  5. [Medium] Polynomial Long Division

    • EN: Divide (x² + 5x + 6) by (x + 2). What is the quotient?
    • FR: Divise (x² + 5x + 6) par (x + 2). Quel est le quotient ?
    • Choices: A) x + 2 B) x + 3 C) x + 4 D) x² + 3
    • Hint: Divide the leading term first, multiply back, subtract, then repeat — like (x² + 4x + 4) ÷ (x + 2): x times (x+2) = x² + 2x, subtract → 2x + 4, divide 2x by x = 2, quotient = x + 2.
    • Steps:
      • x² ÷ x = x. Multiply: x(x + 2) = x² + 2x. Subtract: (x² + 5x + 6) − (x² + 2x) = 3x + 6.
      • 3x ÷ x = 3. Multiply: 3(x + 2) = 3x + 6. Subtract: 0.
      • Quotient = x + 3.
    • Answer: x + 3

  8. [Medium] Binomial Theorem

    • EN: What is the coefficient of x² in the expansion of (x + 2)⁴?
    • FR: Quel est le coefficient de x² dans le développement de (x + 2)⁴ ?
    • Choices: A) 16 B) 24 C) 32 D) 48
    • Hint: Use the binomial theorem: the term with xʳ is C(n, r) × xʳ × bⁿ⁻ʳ — like in (x + 3)³, the x² term is C(3,2) × x² × 3¹ = 3 × 3 × x² = 9x².
    • Steps:
      • The x² term in (x + 2)⁴ comes from r = 2: C(4, 2) × x² × 2².
      • C(4, 2) = 6 and 2² = 4.
      • Coefficient = 6 × 4 = 24.
    • Answer: 24

  9. [Hard] Rational Function — Asymptotes

    • EN: For the rational function f(x) = (2x² + 3) ÷ (x² − 4), what is the horizontal asymptote?
    • FR: Pour la fonction rationnelle f(x) = (2x² + 3) ÷ (x² − 4), quelle est l'asymptote horizontale ?
    • Choices: A) y = 0 B) y = 2 C) y = 3 D) y = 4
    • Hint: When the degree of numerator equals the degree of denominator, the horizontal asymptote is the ratio of leading coefficients — like (5x² + 1) ÷ (2x²) → horizontal asymptote y = 5/2.
    • Steps:
      • Degree of numerator = 2; degree of denominator = 2. Degrees are equal.
      • Horizontal asymptote = leading coefficient of numerator ÷ leading coefficient of denominator.
      • = 2 ÷ 1 = 2. So horizontal asymptote is y = 2.
    • Answer: y = 2

  10. [Hard] Combinations and Probability

    • EN: A committee of 3 people is chosen at random from 4 men and 5 women. What is the probability that all 3 members are women?
    • FR: Un comité de 3 personnes est choisi au hasard parmi 4 hommes et 5 femmes. Quelle est la probabilité que les 3 membres soient des femmes ?
    • Choices: A) 5/42 B) 10/21 C) 10/84 D) 5/21
    • Hint: P = (favourable combos) ÷ (total combos) — like choosing 2 women from 4 women and 3 men: C(4,2)/C(7,2) = 6/21 = 2/7.
    • Steps:
      • Total ways to choose 3 from 9: C(9, 3) = 84.
      • Ways to choose 3 women from 5: C(5, 3) = 10.
      • P = 10/84 = 5/42.
    • Answer: 5/42
