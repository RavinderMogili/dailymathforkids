    • Hint: Velocity is the derivative of position — differentiate s(t) using the power rule, then substitute t, like s(t) = 2t² + t → s'(t) = 4t + 1; at t = 3: s'(3) = 13.
    • Steps:
      • s'(t) = 6t + 2.
      • At t = 4: s'(4) = 6(4) + 2 = 24 + 2 = 26.
      • The velocity is 26 m/s.
    • Answer: 26 m/s

  9. [Hard] Permutations with Restrictions

    • EN: How many 4-digit numbers can be formed from the digits 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 if no digit is repeated and the number must be even?
    • FR: Combien de nombres à 4 chiffres peuvent être formés à partir des chiffres 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 si aucun chiffre ne se répète et si le nombre doit être pair?
    • Choices: A) 36 B) 48 C) 60 D) 72
    • Hint: Fix the last digit first (must be even: 2 or 4), then count arrangements of the remaining digits for the other positions — like if last digit has 2 choices, and 3 remaining spots have 4 × 3 × 2 arrangements.
    • Steps:
      • Even last digit: 2 or 4 → 2 choices.
      • Remaining 3 positions chosen from the 4 remaining digits: 4 × 3 × 2 = 24 ways.
      • Total = 2 × 24 = 48.
    • Answer: 48

  10. [Hard] Binomial Theorem

    • EN: Find the coefficient of x² in the expansion of (x + 3)⁴.
    • FR: Trouve le coefficient de x² dans le développement de (x + 3)⁴.
    • Choices: A) 54 B) 81 C) 108 D) 162
    • Hint: Use the Binomial Theorem: the term with x^k is C(n, k) × x^k × 3^(n–k) — like in (x + 2)³, the x² term is C(3,2) × x² × 2¹ = 3 × 2 × x² = 6x².
    • Steps:
      • In (x + 3)⁴, the x² term corresponds to k = 2: C(4, 2) × x² × 3².
      • C(4, 2) = 6; 3² = 9.
      • Coefficient = 6 × 9 = 54.
    • Answer: 54
