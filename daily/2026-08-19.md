Amara loved spending Saturday mornings at the public library in Yellowknife. One day she noticed an elderly man searching the shelves with a puzzled look, unsure how to find books about Canadian history. Amara smiled and walked him over to the catalogue computer, showing him step by step how to search. The man thanked her warmly, and Amara left the library feeling that helping someone learn was the best kind of adventure.
