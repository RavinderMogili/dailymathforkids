    • Steps:
      • Set the denominator equal to zero: x − 5 = 0.
      • x = 5.
      • The vertical asymptote is x = 5.
    • Answer: x = 5

  9. [Hard] Normal Distribution

    • EN: In a Grade 12 class, exam scores are normally distributed with a mean of 72 and a standard deviation of 8. What is the probability that a randomly chosen student scored between 64 and 80?
    • FR: Dans une classe de 12e année, les notes d'examen suivent une distribution normale avec une moyenne de 72 et un écart type de 8. Quelle est la probabilité qu'un élève ait obtenu entre 64 et 80?
    • Choices: A) 34% B) 50% C) 68% D) 95%
    • Hint: In a normal distribution, approximately 68% of data falls within one standard deviation of the mean (the 68-95-99.7 rule).
    • Steps:
      • 64 = 72 − 8 (one standard deviation below the mean).
      • 80 = 72 + 8 (one standard deviation above the mean).
      • About 68% of values fall within ±1 standard deviation.
    • Answer: 68%

  10. [Hard] Derivative — Tangent Line

    • EN: Find the equation of the tangent line to f(x) = x² + 3x at the point where x = 2.
    • FR: Trouve l'équation de la tangente à f(x) = x² + 3x au point où x = 2.
    • Choices: A) y = 7x − 4 B) y = 7x + 4 C) y = 4x − 7 D) y = 7x − 3
    • Hint: Find f′(x) for the slope, and f(x) for the y-value; use y − y₁ = m(x − x₁) (e.g., f(x) = x² + x, at x = 1: f′(x) = 2x + 1, slope = 3, f(1) = 2, tangent: y = 3x − 1).
    • Steps:
      • f′(x) = 2x + 3; at x = 2: slope m = 2(2) + 3 = 7.
      • f(2) = 4 + 6 = 10.
      • y − 10 = 7(x − 2) → y = 7x − 14 + 10 → y = 7x − 4.
    • Answer: y = 7x − 4
