Grade 1 Problems

  1. [Easy] Counting to Ten

    • EN: There are 7 apples on the table. How many apples are there?
    • FR: Il y a 7 pommes sur la table. Combien y a-t-il de pommes?
    • Choices: A) 5 B) 7 C) 9 D) 6

    • Steps:
      • Count the apples one at a time.
      • 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 — there are 7 apples.
    • Answer: 7

  2. [Easy] Adding Small Numbers
