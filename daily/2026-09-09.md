   - Choices: A) 5  B) 6  C) 7  D) 10
   - Hint: Find the derivative, then substitute the x-value (e.g., for g(x) = 2x² − x, g′(x) = 4x − 1, and g′(3) = 11).
   - Steps:
     - f′(x) = 2x + 3.
     - f′(2) = 2(2) + 3 = 4 + 3 = 7.
     - The instantaneous rate of change at x = 2 is 7.
   - Answer: 7

6. **[Medium] Binomial Probability**
   - EN: A fair coin is flipped 4 times. What is the probability of getting exactly 3 heads?
   - FR: On lance une pièce équilibrée 4 fois. Quelle est la probabilité d'obtenir exactement 3 fois pile?
   - Choices: A) 1/4  B) 3/8  C) 1/4  D) 3/16
   - Hint: Use P(X = k) = C(n, k) × p^k × (1−p)^(n−k) (e.g., 3 flips, 2 heads: C(3,2) × (0.5)² × (0.5)¹ = 3 × 0.25 × 0.5 = 0.375).
   - Steps:
     - P(X = 3) = C(4, 3) × (0.5)³ × (0.5)¹.
     - C(4, 3) = 4.
     - P = 4 × 0.125 × 0.5 = 4 × 0.0625 = 0.25 = 1/4.
   - Answer: 1/4

7. **[Medium] Vectors — Addition**
   - EN: Vector **u** = (3, −2) and vector **v** = (−1, 5). What is **u** + **v**?
   - FR: Le vecteur **u** = (3, −2) et le vecteur **v** = (−1, 5). Combien fait **u** + **v**?
   - Choices: A) (2, 3)  B) (3, 2)  C) (4, 3)  D) (2, −3)
   - Hint: Add vectors component by component (e.g., (4, 1) + (−2, 6) = (4+(−2), 1+6) = (2, 7)).
   - Steps:
     - Add x-components: 3 + (−1) = 2.
     - Add y-components: −2 + 5 = 3.
     - **u** + **v** = (2, 3).
   - Answer: (2, 3)

8. **[Medium] Polynomial Long Division**
   - EN: Divide x² + 5x + 6 by (x + 2). What is the quotient?
   - FR: Divise x² + 5x + 6 par (x + 2). Quel est le quotient?
   - Choices: A) x + 2  B) x + 3  C) x − 3  D) x + 4
   - Hint: Divide the leading term of the dividend by the leading term of the divisor, multiply, subtract, and repeat (e.g., (x² + 6x + 8) ÷ (x + 4) = x + 2).
   - Steps:
     - x² ÷ x = x; x(x + 2) = x² + 2x; subtract to get 3x + 6.
     - 3x ÷ x = 3; 3(x + 2) = 3x + 6; subtract to get 0.
     - Quotient is x + 3.
   - Answer: x + 3

9. **[Hard] Normal Distribution**
   - EN: Test scores are normally distributed with a mean of 70 and a standard deviation of 10. Approximately what percentage of students scored between 60 and 80?
   - FR: Les scores à un test suivent une distribution normale avec une moyenne de 70 et un écart-type de 10. Quel pourcentage approximatif d'élèves a obtenu entre 60 et 80?
   - Choices: A) 34%  B) 50%  C) 68%  D) 95%
   - Hint: In a normal distribution, about 68% of data falls within one standard deviation of the mean (e.g., mean 50, SD 5: approximately 68% of scores fall between 45 and 55).
   - Steps:
     - 60 = 70 − 10 = one SD below the mean.
     - 80 = 70 + 10 = one SD above the mean.
     - By the 68-95-99.7 rule, approximately 68% of students fall within one SD of the mean.
   - Answer: 68%

10. **[Hard] Derivative Application — Optimization**
    - EN: A farmer has 120 m of fencing to enclose a rectangular garden against a barn wall (the wall forms one side and needs no fencing). What width (in metres) maximizes the area?
    - FR: Un fermier a 120 m de clôture pour entourer un jardin rectangulaire contre un mur de grange (le mur forme un côté et n'a pas besoin de clôture). Quelle largeur (en mètres) maximise l'aire?
    - Choices: A) 20 m  B) 30 m  C) 40 m  D) 60 m
    - Hint: Write the area as a function of one variable, differentiate, and set equal to zero (e.g., if fencing = 80 m against a wall: A = w(80 − 2w) = 80w − 2w², A′ = 80 − 4w = 0, w = 20 m).
    - Steps:
      - Let width = w and length = 120 − 2w (one wall side covered).
      - Area: A(w) = w(120 − 2w) = 120w − 2w².
      - A′(w) = 120 − 4w = 0, so w = 30 m.
    - Answer: 30 m
