## Mini Story of Kindness

On a snowy school morning in Sudbury, a girl named Amara noticed her elderly neighbour struggling to walk down his icy front steps. She carefully took his arm and guided him to the sidewalk, making sure he was steady. Then she quickly salted the steps before running to catch her school bus. Amara arrived at school with cold cheeks and a warm heart, knowing she had made someone's morning a little safer.
