    • Choices: A) 0.25 B) 0.3125 C) 0.375 D) 0.5
    • Hint: P(X=k) = C(n,k) × pᵏ × (1–p)ⁿ⁻ᵏ — like n=4, k=2, p=0.5: C(4,2)×0.25×0.25 = 6×0.0625 = 0.375.
    • Steps:
      • P(X=3) = C(5,3) × (0.5)³ × (0.5)².
      • C(5,3) = 10.
      • = 10 × 0.125 × 0.25 = 10 × 0.03125 = 0.3125.
    • Answer: 0.3125

  10. [Hard] Derivative — Optimization

    • EN: A farmer wants to fence a rectangular field along a river (no fence on the river side). He has 120 m of fencing for the three other sides. What width maximizes the area of the field?
    • FR: Un agriculteur veut clôturer un champ rectangulaire le long d'une rivière (pas de clôture du côté de la rivière). Il a 120 m de clôture pour les trois autres côtés. Quelle largeur maximise l'aire du champ?
    • Choices: A) 20 m B) 30 m C) 40 m D) 60 m
    • Hint: Let width = w (two sides) and length = l; then 2w + l = total fencing, so l = total – 2w; area = w×l; find derivative and set to zero — like 80 m fencing: 2w+l=80, A=w(80–2w)=80w–2w², A'=80–4w=0, w=20.
    • Steps:
      • Let width = w; length = 120 – 2w.
      • Area A(w) = w(120 – 2w) = 120w – 2w².
      • A'(w) = 120 – 4w = 0.
      • 4w = 120, w = 30 m.
    • Answer: 30 m
