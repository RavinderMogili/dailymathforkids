Grade 1 Problems

  1. [Easy] Counting to 20

    • EN: There are 7 red apples and 5 green apples in a basket. How many apples are there in all?
    • FR: Il y a 7 pommes rouges et 5 pommes vertes dans un panier. Combien y a-t-il de pommes en tout?
    • Choices: A) 10 B) 11 C) 12 D) 13
    • Hint: To add two numbers, count on from the bigger number — try 8 + 3.
    • Steps:
      • Start at 7, the bigger number.
      • Count on 5 more: 8, 9, 10, 11, 12.
      • So the total is 12.
    • Answer: 12

  2. [Easy] Subtraction Within 20
