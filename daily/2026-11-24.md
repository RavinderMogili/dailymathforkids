   - FR: Quelle est la dérivée de f(x) = sin(2x)?

9. **[Hard] Optimization with Calculus**
   - EN: A rectangular garden has a perimeter of 40 m. What is the maximum area?
   - FR: Un jardin rectangulaire a un périmètre de 40 m. Quelle est la surface maximale?
   - Choices: A) 100 m²  B) 120 m²  C) 150 m²  D) 160 m²
   - Hint: A rectangle with perimeter 30 m; maximum area.
   - Steps:
     - Let length = x, width = (20 - x)
     - Area = x(20 - x) = 20x - x²
     - Take derivative: dA/dx = 20 - 2x = 0 → x = 10
     - Max area = 10 × 10 = 100
   - Answer: 100 m²

10. **[Hard] Probability with Combinations**

    - FR: Un comité de 3 est choisi parmi 5 étudiants. Combien de comités possibles?
    - Choices: A) 10  B) 15  C) 20  D) 25
    - Hint: How many ways to choose 2 from 4 students?
    - Steps:
      - Use combination formula: C(n, k) = n! / (k!(n - k)!)

    - Answer: 10
