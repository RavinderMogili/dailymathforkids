   - Choices: A) (1, 8)  B) (2, 9)  C) (3, 8)  D) (4, 9)
   - Hint: Add corresponding components — for example, (2, 3) + (5, 1) = (7, 4).
   - Steps:
     - Add x-components: 3 + (−1) = 2.
     - Add y-components: 4 + 5 = 9.
     - **a** + **b** = (2, 9).
   - Answer: (2, 9)

9. **[Hard] Derivatives — Optimization**
   - EN: A Canadian farmer wants to fence a rectangular area of 144 m² using the least amount of fencing. What is the minimum perimeter?
   - FR: Un fermier canadien veut clôturer une aire rectangulaire de 144 m² en utilisant le moins de clôture possible. Quel est le périmètre minimal?
   - Choices: A) 36 m  B) 40 m  C) 44 m  D) 48 m
   - Hint: For a fixed area, the rectangle with minimum perimeter is a square — find the side length by taking the square root of the area, then calculate the perimeter; for example, area = 36 m²: side = 6, P = 24 m.
   - Steps:
     - For minimum perimeter with fixed area, use a square.
     - Side = √144 = 12 m.
     - Perimeter = 4 × 12 = 48 m.
   - Answer: 48 m

10. **[Hard] Normal Distribution**
    - EN: The heights of Grade 12 students at a school are normally distributed with a mean of 170 cm and a standard deviation of 10 cm. Approximately what percentage of students are between 160 cm and 180 cm tall?
    - FR: Les tailles des élèves de 12e année dans une école suivent une distribution normale de moyenne 170 cm et d'écart-type 10 cm. Environ quel pourcentage d'élèves mesurent entre 160 cm et 180 cm?
    - Choices: A) 50%  B) 64%  C) 68%  D) 95%
    - Hint: The empirical rule states that about 68% of data falls within one standard deviation of the mean — for example, mean = 50, SD = 5: about 68% are between 45 and 55.
    - Steps:
      - 160 = 170 − 10 (one SD below); 180 = 170 + 10 (one SD above).
      - The range 160–180 is within one standard deviation.
      - By the empirical rule, approximately 68% of students are in this range.
    - Answer: 68%
