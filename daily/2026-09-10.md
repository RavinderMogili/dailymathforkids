## Mini Story of Kindness

Priya lived in a cozy house in Saskatoon with her grandmother, Nani. One afternoon, Nani asked for help reaching the jars of spices on the high kitchen shelf. Priya carefully climbed on a step stool and handed each jar down one by one, even labelling them in large letters so Nani could read them easily. Nani smiled and said, "You are the best helper I know," and they spent the evening making dal together.
