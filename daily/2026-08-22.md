   - Hint: To evaluate a polynomial, substitute the value for x and follow BEDMAS — for f(x) = x² − 3x + 1 at x = 4: 16 − 12 + 1 = 5.
   - Steps:
     - f(2) = 2³ − 2(2) + 5.
     - = 8 − 4 + 5.
     - = 9.
     - So f(2) = 9.
   - Answer: 9

3. **[Easy] Combinations**
   - EN: A student must choose 2 courses from a list of 5. How many combinations are possible?
   - FR: Un élève doit choisir 2 cours parmi une liste de 5. Combien de combinaisons sont possibles?
   - Choices: A) 8  B) 10  C) 12  D) 20
   - Hint: The combination formula is C(n, r) = n! ÷ (r! × (n − r)!) — for choosing 2 from 4: C(4,2) = 6.
   - Steps:
     - C(5, 2) = 5! ÷ (2! × 3!) = (5 × 4) ÷ (2 × 1).
     - = 20 ÷ 2 = 10.
     - So there are 10 combinations.
   - Answer: 10

4. **[Easy] Rate of Change — Intro to Derivatives**
   - EN: The position of a car is given by s(t) = 3t², where t is in seconds and s is in metres. What is the average rate of change between t = 1 and t = 3?
   - FR: La position d'une voiture est donnée par s(t) = 3t², où t est en secondes et s est en mètres. Quel est le taux de variation moyen entre t = 1 et t = 3?
   - Choices: A) 6 m/s  B) 10 m/s  C) 12 m/s  D) 15 m/s
   - Hint: Average rate of change = (f(b) − f(a)) ÷ (b − a) — like for f(t) = 2t² from t = 1 to t = 2: (8 − 2)/(2 − 1) = 6 m/s.
   - Steps:
     - s(1) = 3(1)² = 3 m.
     - s(3) = 3(3)² = 27 m.
     - Average rate of change = (27 − 3)/(3 − 1) = 24/2 = 12 m/s.
   - Answer: 12 m/s

5. **[Medium] Derivative — Power Rule**
   - EN: Find the derivative of f(x) = 4x³ − 5x + 7.
   - FR: Trouve la dérivée de f(x) = 4x³ − 5x + 7.
   - Choices: A) 12x² − 5  B) 4x² − 5  C) 12x² + 7  D) 12x³ − 5
   - Hint: The power rule states d/dx [x^n] = n·x^(n−1) — for 3x⁴ − 2x + 1, the derivative is 12x³ − 2.
   - Steps:
     - Derivative of 4x³ = 4 × 3x² = 12x².
     - Derivative of −5x = −5.
     - Derivative of 7 (constant) = 0.
     - So f'(x) = 12x² − 5.
   - Answer: 12x² − 5

6. **[Medium] Probability Distribution**
   - EN: A fair six-sided die is rolled twice. What is the probability that both rolls show a number greater than 4?
   - FR: On lance un dé équilibré à six faces deux fois. Quelle est la probabilité que les deux lancers donnent un nombre supérieur à 4?
   - Choices: A) 1/9  B) 1/6  C) 2/9  D) 1/3
   - Hint: For independent events, P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B) — like rolling a 6 twice: (1/6) × (1/6) = 1/36.
   - Steps:
     - Numbers greater than 4 on a die: 5 and 6 — so 2 out of 6.
     - P(roll > 4) = 2/6 = 1/3.
     - P(both > 4) = 1/3 × 1/3 = 1/9.
   - Answer: 1/9

7. **[Medium] Rational Function — Finding Asymptote**
   - EN: For the rational function f(x) = (2x + 3)/(x − 4), what is the equation of the vertical asymptote?
   - FR: Pour la fonction rationnelle f(x) = (2x + 3)/(x − 4), quelle est l'équation de l'asymptote verticale?
   - Choices: A) x = −3  B) x = 2  C) x = 4  D) x = 3
   - Hint: A vertical asymptote occurs where the denominator equals zero — for (x + 2)/(x − 5), set x − 5 = 0 to get x = 5.
   - Steps:
     - Set the denominator equal to zero: x − 4 = 0.
     - x = 4.
     - So the vertical asymptote is x = 4.
   - Answer: x = 4

8. **[Medium] Vectors — Adding Vectors**
   - EN: Vector **a** = (3, −2) and vector **b** = (−1, 5). What is **a** + **b**?
   - FR: Le vecteur **a** = (3, −2) et le vecteur **b** = (−1, 5). Combien font **a** + **b**?
   - Choices: A) (2, 3)  B) (4, 3)  C) (2, 7)  D) (4, −7)
   - Hint: To add vectors, add the corresponding components — like (2, 5) + (3, −1) = (5, 4).
   - Steps:
     - Add x-components: 3 + (−1) = 2.
     - Add y-components: −2 + 5 = 3.
     - So **a** + **b** = (2, 3).
   - Answer: (2, 3)

9. **[Hard] Derivative — Application (Tangent Line)**
   - EN: Find the equation of the tangent line to f(x) = x² + 3x at the point where x = 2.
   - FR: Trouve l'équation de la droite tangente à f(x) = x² + 3x au point où x = 2.
   - Choices: A) y = 7x − 4  B) y = 7x + 4  C) y = 7x − 6  D) y = 5x + 4
   - Hint: The tangent line at a point uses slope = f'(a) and the point (a, f(a)) in y − y₁ = m(x − x₁) — find the derivative first.
   - Steps:
     - Find f(2): f(2) = 4 + 6 = 10. Point: (2, 10).
     - Find f'(x): f'(x) = 2x + 3.
     - f'(2) = 2(2) + 3 = 7. Slope = 7.
     - Equation: y − 10 = 7(x − 2) → y = 7x − 14 + 10 → y = 7x − 4.
   - Answer: y = 7x − 4

10. **[Hard] Binomial Theorem**
    - EN: What is the coefficient of x² in the expansion of (x + 3)⁴?
    - FR: Quel est le coefficient de x² dans le développement de (x + 3)⁴?
    - Choices: A) 27  B) 54  C) 81  D) 108
    - Hint: Use the Binomial Theorem: the term with x^k in (x + a)^n is C(n, k) × x^k × a^(n−k) — identify k for the term you need.
    - Steps:
      - For (x + 3)⁴, the general term is C(4, k) × x^k × 3^(4−k).
      - For the x² term, k = 2: C(4, 2) × x² × 3².
      - C(4, 2) = 6; 3² = 9.
      - Coefficient = 6 × 9 = 54.
    - Answer: 54
