Grade 1 Problems

  1. [Easy] Counting to 20

    • EN: There are 7 red apples and 5 green apples in a basket. How many apples are there in all?
    • FR: Il y a 7 pommes rouges et 5 pommes vertes dans un panier. Combien y a-t-il de pommes en tout?
    • Choices: A) 10 B) 11 C) 12 D) 13
    • Hint: Add the two groups together, like 3 + 4 = 7.
    • Steps:
      • Start at 7 and count on 5 more.
      • 7 … 8, 9, 10, 11, 12. There are 12 apples in all.
    • Answer: 12

  2. [Easy] Skip Counting by 2s

    • EN: Skip count by 2s. What number comes next: 2, 4, 6, 8, ___?
    • FR: Compte par bonds de 2. Quel nombre vient ensuite : 2, 4, 6, 8, ___?

    • Hint: Skip counting by 2s means adding 2 each time, like 10, 12, 14.
    • Steps:
      • Each number goes up by 2.
      • 8 + 2 = 10, so 10 comes next.
    • Answer: 10
