    • EN: In how many ways can 4 students be arranged in a line?
    • FR: De combien de façons peut-on placer 4 élèves dans une file?
    • Choices: A) 16 B) 20 C) 24 D) 32
    • Hint: The number of arrangements of n items is n! = n × (n−1) × … × 1 (e.g., 3 students: 3! = 3 × 2 × 1 = 6).
    • Steps:
      • 4! = 4 × 3 × 2 × 1.
      • 4 × 3 = 12; 12 × 2 = 24; 24 × 1 = 24.
    • Answer: 24

  2. [Easy] Combinations

    • EN: How many ways can you choose 2 books from a shelf of 5?
    • FR: De combien de façons peut-on choisir 2 livres parmi 5?
    • Choices: A) 8 B) 10 C) 12 D) 20
    • Hint: Use C(n, r) = n! / (r! × (n−r)!) (e.g., C(4,2) = 4!/(2!×2!) = 24/4 = 6).
    • Steps:
      • C(5, 2) = 5! / (2! × 3!) = (5 × 4) / (2 × 1).
      • 20 / 2 = 10.
    • Answer: 10

    • FR: Si f(x) = x³, quelle est f′(x)?

    • Hint: Power rule: bring the exponent down as a coefficient and reduce the exponent by 1 (e.g., f(x) = x⁴ → f′(x) = 4x³).
    • Steps:
      • Bring down the exponent: coefficient = 3.
      • Reduce exponent: 3 − 1 = 2.
      • f′(x) = 3x².
