   - Choices: A) 6(2x + 1)^2  B) 3(2x + 1)^2  C) 2(2x + 1)^3  D) 6(2x + 1)^3
   - Hint: Apply the chain rule: derivative of outer function times derivative of inner function.
   - Steps:
     - Step 1: Let u = 2x + 1, so f(u) = u³.
     - Step 2: Find the derivative of f(u): df/du = 3u².
     - Step 3: Find the derivative of u with respect to x: du/dx = 2.
     - Step 4: Multiply the derivatives: df/dx = 3u² * 2 = 6u² = 6(2x + 1)^2.
   - Answer: 6(2x + 1)^2
