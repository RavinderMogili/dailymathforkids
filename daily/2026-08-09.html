    • EN: There are 7 apples in a basket. Then 5 more apples are added. How many apples are there in all?
    • FR: Il y a 7 pommes dans un panier. Ensuite, 5 autres pommes sont ajoutées. Combien y a-t-il de pommes en tout?

    • Hint: To add, count on from the bigger number — try 8 + 4: start at 8 and count up 4 more.
    • Steps:
      • Start at 7 and count on 5 more: 8, 9, 10, 11, 12.
      • So 7 + 5 = 12 apples.
    • Answer: 12 apples
