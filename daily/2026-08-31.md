   - Steps:
     - f(2) = (2)³ − 3(2)² + 2(2) − 5.
     - = 8 − 12 + 4 − 5.
     - = −5.
   - Answer: −5

10. **[Hard] Probability — Conditional**
    - EN: In a class of 30 students, 18 play hockey and 12 play soccer. 6 students play both sports. If a student is selected at random and plays hockey, what is the probability that they also play soccer?
    - FR: Dans une classe de 30 élèves, 18 jouent au hockey et 12 jouent au soccer. 6 élèves jouent aux deux sports. Si un élève est choisi au hasard parmi ceux qui jouent au hockey, quelle est la probabilité qu'il joue aussi au soccer?
    - Choices: A) 1/6  B) 1/5  C) 1/3  D) 2/5
    - Hint: Conditional probability P(B|A) = P(A and B) / P(A) (e.g., 10 play chess, 4 also play checkers: P(checkers|chess) = 4/10 = 2/5).
    - Steps:
      - Students who play hockey: 18.
      - Students who play both hockey and soccer: 6.
      - P(soccer | hockey) = 6 ÷ 18 = 1/3.
    - Answer: 1/3
