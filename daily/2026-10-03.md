    - Choices: A) z = 1, slightly above average  B) z = 2, two standard deviations above the mean  C) z = 4, four standard deviations above the mean  D) z = 16, well above average
    - Hint: z = (x − μ) / σ — e.g., score 85, mean 70, SD 5: z = (85−70)/5 = 3, meaning 3 SDs above the mean.
    - Steps:
      - z = (88 − 72) / 8 = 16 / 8 = 2.
      - A z-score of 2 means the student scored 2 standard deviations above the mean.
      - The answer is z = 2, two standard deviations above the mean.
    - Answer: z = 2, two standard deviations above the mean

## Mini Story of Kindness

Aisha loved spending Saturday mornings at the Riverview Community Garden in Moncton. One afternoon, she noticed her elderly neighbour, Mr. Gauthier, struggling to water the tall tomato plants at the far end of the plot. Without being asked, Aisha carried the heavy watering can back and forth until every plant had a long, cool drink. Mr. Gauthier smiled and thanked her, then showed her how to spot the tiny green tomatoes hiding behind the leaves, and Aisha thought that was the best reward of all.
