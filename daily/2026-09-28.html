Grade 1 Problems

  1. [Easy] Counting to 20

    • EN: There are 7 apples on the table and 5 more apples in the basket. How many apples are there in all?
    • FR: Il y a 7 pommes sur la table et 5 autres pommes dans le panier. Combien y a-t-il de pommes en tout?
    • Choices: A) 10 apples B) 11 apples C) 12 apples D) 13 apples
    • Hint: To add 6 + 4, count on from 6: 7, 8, 9, 10 — that is 4 jumps, so the answer is 10.
    • Steps:
      • Start at 7 and count on 5 more: 8, 9, 10, 11, 12.
      • We counted 5 jumps, so 7 + 5 = 12.
    • Answer: 12 apples
