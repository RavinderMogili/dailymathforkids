    • EN: Find the dot product of u = (2, 5) and v = (4, −3).
    • FR: Trouve le produit scalaire de u = (2, 5) et v = (4, −3).
    • Choices: A) −7 B) −5 C) 5 D) 23
    • Hint: Dot product = (a × c) + (b × d) where u = (a, b) and v = (c, d) (e.g., (3, 2) · (4, −1) = 12 + (−2) = 10).
    • Steps:
      • (2)(4) = 8.
      • (5)(−3) = −15.
      • Dot product = 8 + (−15) = −7.
    • Answer: −7
