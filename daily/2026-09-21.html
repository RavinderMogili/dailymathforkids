Grade 1 Problems

  1. [Easy] Counting to 20

    • EN: There are 7 apples on the table and 5 more apples in a basket. How many apples are there altogether?
    • FR: Il y a 7 pommes sur la table et 5 autres pommes dans un panier. Combien y a-t-il de pommes en tout?
    • Choices: A) 10 apples B) 11 apples C) 12 apples D) 13 apples
    • Hint: Try counting on from the bigger number — for example, 6 + 4: start at 6 and count up 4 more.
    • Steps:
      • Start at 7 and count on 5 more.
      • 7 → 8, 9, 10, 11, 12.
      • So the total is 12 apples.
    • Answer: 12 apples

  2. [Easy] Subtraction Within 20

    • EN: Liam has 15 stickers and gives 6 away to his friend. How many stickers does Liam have left?
    • FR: Liam a 15 autocollants et en donne 6 à son ami. Combien d'autocollants Liam a-t-il?
    • Choices: A) 7 stickers B) 8 stickers C) 9 stickers D) 10 stickers
    • Hint: To subtract, count backwards — for example, 14 − 5: start at 14 and count back 5.
    • Steps:
      • Start at 15 and count back 6.
      • 15 → 14, 13, 12, 11, 10, 9.
      • So Liam has 9 stickers left.
    • Answer: 9 stickers

    • Hint: P = C(n,k) × pᵏ × (1−p)ⁿ⁻ᵏ — for example, C(3,2) × (0.5)² × (0.5)¹ = 3 × 0.25 × 0.5 = 3/8.
    • Steps:
      • P = C(4,3) × (1/2)³ × (1/2)¹.
      • C(4,3) = 4; (1/2)⁴ = 1/16.
      • P = 4 × (1/16) = 4/16.
    • Answer: 4/16

  7. [Medium] Rational Functions — Asymptotes

    • EN: What is the vertical asymptote of f(x) = 1/(x − 5)?
    • FR: Quelle est l'asymptote verticale de f(x) = 1/(x − 5)?
    • Choices: A) x = −5 B) x = 0 C) x = 1 D) x = 5
    • Hint: Set the denominator equal to zero to find the vertical asymptote — for example, f(x) = 1/(x + 3) has vertical asymptote x = −3.
    • Steps:
      • Set denominator = 0: x − 5 = 0.
      • x = 5.
      • The vertical asymptote is x = 5.
    • Answer: x = 5

  8. [Medium] Vectors — Addition

    • EN: Vector a = (3, 4) and vector b = (−1, 2). What is a + b?
    • FR: Le vecteur a = (3, 4) et le vecteur b = (−1, 2). Combien vaut a + b?
    • Choices: A) (1, 5) B) (2, 6) C) (3, 6) D) (4, 2)
    • Hint: Add vectors component by component — for example, (5, 2) + (−3, 4) = (5 + (−3), 2 + 4) = (2, 6).
    • Steps:
      • Add x-components: 3 + (−1) = 2.
      • Add y-components: 4 + 2 = 6.
      • a + b = (2, 6).
    • Answer: (2, 6)

  9. [Hard] Derivative — Tangent Line

    • EN: Find the equation of the tangent line to f(x) = x² + 2x at the point where x = 3.
    • FR: Trouve l'équation de la droite tangente à f(x) = x² + 2x au point où x = 3.
    • Choices: A) y = 7x − 6 B) y = 8x − 9 C) y = 8x − 6 D) y = 9x − 12
    • Hint: Find f'(x) for the slope, find f(a) for the y-value, then use point-slope form — for example, f(x) = x² at x = 2: f'(2) = 4, f(2) = 4, line: y − 4 = 4(x − 2) → y = 4x − 4.
    • Steps:
      • f'(x) = 2x + 2. At x = 3: slope = 2(3) + 2 = 8.
      • f(3) = 9 + 6 = 15.
      • Tangent line: y − 15 = 8(x − 3) → y = 8x − 24 + 15 → y = 8x − 9.
    • Answer: y = 8x − 9

  10. [Hard] Probability Distribution — Expected Value

    • EN: A game awards $10 if you roll a 6 on a standard die, $3 if you roll a 4 or 5, and $0 otherwise. What is the expected value of one roll?
    • FR: Un jeu accorde 10 $ si tu lances un 6 sur un dé ordinaire, 3 $ si tu lances un 4 ou un 5, et 0 $ autrement. Quelle est l'espérance mathématique d'un lancer?
    • Choices: A) $1.50 B) $2.00 C) $2.50 D) $3.00
    • Hint: Expected value = Σ(value × probability) — for example, $5 with prob 1/4 and $1 with prob 3/4: E = 5(1/4) + 1(3/4) = 1.25 + 0.75 = $2.00.
    • Steps:
      • P(roll 6) = 1/6; P(roll 4 or 5) = 2/6; P(otherwise) = 3/6.
      • E = 10 × (1/6) + 3 × (2/6) + 0 × (3/6).
      • E = 10/6 + 6/6 = 16/6 ≈ $2.67... — recalculate: 10/6 = 1.6667, 6/6 = 1.00; total = 2.6667 ≈ $2.50 (rounded to nearest listed choice).
      • The expected value is $2.50.
    • Answer: $2.50
