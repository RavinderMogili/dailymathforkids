Grade 1 Problems

  1. [Easy] Counting Apples

    • EN: Maya has 7 apples. She picks 5 more. How many apples does she have now?
    • FR: Maya a 7 pommes. Elle en cueille 5 autres. Combien de pommes a-t-elle maintenant?
    • Choices: A) 10 B) 11 C) 12 D) 13

      • Start at 7, which is the bigger number.
      • Count up 5 more: 8, 9, 10, 11, 12.
      • So Maya has 12 apples.
    • Answer: 12

    • FR: La position d'une particule (en mètres) est donnée par s(t) = t³ − 6t² + 9t, où t est en secondes. Quelle est la vitesse de la particule à t = 4 s?
    • Choices: A) 4 m/s B) 7 m/s C) 9 m/s D) 16 m/s
    • Hint: Velocity = derivative of position. Find v(t) = s′(t), then evaluate at the given time. For example, s(t) = t² − 4t: v(t) = 2t − 4; at t = 3, v = 2.
    • Steps:
      • v(t) = s′(t) = 3t² − 12t + 9.
      • At t = 4: v(4) = 3(16) − 12(4) + 9 = 48 − 48 + 9.
      • v(4) = 9 m/s.
    • Answer: 9 m/s
