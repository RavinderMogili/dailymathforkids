Grade 1 Problems

  1. [Easy] Counting Pucks

    • EN: There are 7 hockey pucks on the ice. 3 more pucks slide over. How many pucks are there now?
    • FR: Il y a 7 rondelles de hockey sur la glace. 3 autres rondelles arrivent. Combien y a-t-il de rondelles maintenant?
    • Choices: A) 8 pucks B) 9 pucks C) 10 pucks D) 11 pucks
    • Hint: To add, count on from the bigger number — for example, 5 + 2: start at 5 and count 2 more.
    • Steps:
      • Start at 7 and count on 3 more.
      • 7 → 8, 9, 10.
      • So there are 10 pucks in all.
    • Answer: 10 pucks

  2. [Easy] Pennies in a Jar

      • Start at 6, which is the bigger number.

      • So Maya has 10 pennies.
    • Answer: 10 pennies
