Grade 1 Problems

  1. [Easy] Counting to 20

    • EN: There are 7 apples on the table and 5 more in a basket. How many apples are there in total?
    • FR: Il y a 7 pommes sur la table et 5 de plus dans un panier. Combien y a-t-il de pommes en tout?
    • Choices: A) 11 B) 12 C) 13 D) 14
    • Hint: Try counting on from the bigger number, like 8 + 4 — start at 8 and count up 4.
    • Steps:
      • Start at 7 and count on 5 more: 8, 9, 10, 11, 12.
      • So 7 + 5 = 12.
    • Answer: 12

  2. [Easy] Skip Counting by 2s

    • EN: Skip count by 2s. What number comes next: 4, 6, 8, ___?
    • FR: Compte par bonds de 2. Quel nombre vient ensuite : 4, 6, 8, ___?
    • Choices: A) 9 B) 10 C) 11 D) 12
    • Hint: When skip counting by 2s, like 2, 4, 6 — just add 2 each time.
    • Steps:
      • The pattern adds 2 each time: 4 + 2 = 6, 6 + 2 = 8.
      • 8 + 2 = 10.
    • Answer: 10

  3. [Easy] Comparing Numbers

    • EN: Which number is bigger: 14 or 9?
    • FR: Quel nombre est plus grand : 14 ou 9?
    • Choices: A) 9 B) 11 C) 14 D) 16
    • Hint: Think about counting — numbers that come later are bigger, just like 6 is bigger than 3.
    • Steps:
      • 14 is a two-digit number; 9 is a one-digit number.
      • Two-digit numbers are always greater than one-digit numbers.
      • So 14 is bigger.
    • Answer: 14

    • EN: A pair of mittens has 2 mittens. How many mittens are in 6 pairs?
    • FR: Une paire de mitaines contient 2 mitaines. Combien de mitaines y a-t-il dans 6 paires?

    • FR: Il y a 5 joueurs dans chaque équipe de basketball. Combien de joueurs y a-t-il dans 4 équipes?
    • Choices: A) 15 B) 20 C) 25 D) 30
