    • EN: Maya has 7 teddy bears on her shelf. She gets 5 more. How many teddy bears does she have now?
    • FR: Maya a 7 oursons sur son étagère. Elle en reçoit 5 de plus. Combien d'oursons a-t-elle maintenant?
    • Choices: A) 10 bears B) 11 bears C) 12 bears D) 13 bears

    • Steps:
      • Start at 7 and count up 5 more.
      • 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 — that is 5 steps.
      • So Maya has 12 teddy bears.
    • Answer: 12 bears
