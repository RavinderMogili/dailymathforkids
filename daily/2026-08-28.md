     - **a** + **b** = (2, 3).
   - Answer: (2, 3)

7. **[Medium] Rational Functions — Vertical Asymptote**
   - EN: For the function f(x) = (x + 2)/(x − 5), where is the vertical asymptote?
   - FR: Pour la fonction f(x) = (x + 2)/(x − 5), où se trouve l'asymptote verticale?
   - Choices: A) x = −2  B) x = 2  C) x = 5  D) x = 7
   - Hint: Vertical asymptotes occur where the denominator equals zero (e.g., f(x) = 1/(x − 3): vertical asymptote at x = 3).
   - Steps:
     - Set the denominator equal to zero: x − 5 = 0.
     - x = 5.
     - The vertical asymptote is at x = 5.
   - Answer: x = 5

8. **[Medium] Permutations with Restrictions**
   - EN: How many 3-letter arrangements can be made from the letters A, B, C, D, E if no letter is repeated?
   - FR: Combien de mots de 3 lettres peut-on former avec les lettres A, B, C, D, E si aucune lettre n'est répétée?
   - Choices: A) 30  B) 45  C) 60  D) 75
   - Hint: This is a permutation P(n, r) = n!/(n−r)! (e.g., P(4, 2) = 4!/2! = 24/2 = 12 arrangements).
   - Steps:
     - P(5, 3) = 5!/(5 − 3)! = 5!/2!.
     - = (5 × 4 × 3 × 2!)/2! = 5 × 4 × 3.
     - = 60 arrangements.
   - Answer: 60

9. **[Hard] Derivative Application — Instantaneous Rate of Change**
   - EN: The position of a hockey puck is given by s(t) = 3t² − 8t + 5 (in metres, t in seconds). What is the instantaneous velocity at t = 4 seconds?
   - FR: La position d'une rondelle de hockey est donnée par s(t) = 3t² − 8t + 5 (en mètres, t en secondes). Quelle est la vitesse instantanée à t = 4 secondes?
   - Choices: A) 12 m/s  B) 16 m/s  C) 20 m/s  D) 24 m/s
   - Hint: Find v(t) = s'(t) using the power rule, then substitute t (e.g., s(t) = 2t² − 6t: s'(t) = 4t − 6; at t = 3: v = 12 − 6 = 6 m/s).
   - Steps:
     - s'(t) = 6t − 8.
     - At t = 4: v = 6(4) − 8.
     - v = 24 − 8 = 16 m/s.
   - Answer: 16 m/s

10. **[Hard] Binomial Theorem**
    - EN: Find the coefficient of the x² term in the expansion of (x + 3)⁴.
    - FR: Trouve le coefficient du terme en x² dans le développement de (x + 3)⁴.
    - Choices: A) 27  B) 54  C) 81  D) 108
    - Hint: Use the binomial theorem: the term with xᵏ is C(n, k) × xᵏ × (constant)ⁿ⁻ᵏ (e.g., in (x + 2)³, the x term: C(3,1) × x × 2² = 3 × 4x = 12x, coefficient = 12).
    - Steps:
      - We need the x² term in (x + 3)⁴; this means k = 2.
      - Term = C(4, 2) × x² × 3² = 6 × x² × 9.
      - Coefficient = 6 × 9 = 54.
    - Answer: 54
