    • FR: Trouvez l'inverse de f(x) = 2x + 7.
    • Choices: A) (x - 7)/2 B) (x + 7)/2 C) 2(x - 7) D) 2(x + 7)
    • Hint: Swap x and y, solve for y.
    • Steps:
      • y = 2x + 7; x = 2y + 7; solve for y.
    • Answer: (x - 7)/2 B) (x + 7)/2 C) 2(x - 7) D) 2(x + 7)

  7. [Medium] Understanding Polynomial Functions

    • EN: What is the degree of the polynomial 5x³ - 4x² + 2x - 1?
    • FR: Quel est le degré du polynôme 5x³ - 4x² + 2x - 1 ?
    • Choices: A) 1 B) 2 C) 3 D) 4
    • Hint: Highest exponent is the degree.
    • Steps:
      • Highest power in polynomial: x³.
    • Answer: 3

  8. [Medium] Combinations and Permutations

    • EN: How many ways to choose 2 letters from A, B, C, D?
    • FR: Combien de façons de choisir 2 lettres parmi A, B, C, D ?
    • Choices: A) 4 B) 6 C) 8 D) 10
    • Hint: Use combinations formula C(n, k).
    • Steps:
      • C(4, 2) = 4! / (2!(4-2)!) = 6.
    • Answer: 6

  9. [Hard] Applied Derivatives

    • EN: Find the slope of f(x) = x² + 1 at x = 2.
    • FR: Trouvez la pente de f(x) = x² + 1 à x = 2.
    • Choices: A) 2 B) 4 C) 5 D) 8
    • Hint: Differentiate, then plug in value.
    • Steps:
      • Derivative f'(x) = 2x; at x = 2, slope = 4.
    • Answer: 4
