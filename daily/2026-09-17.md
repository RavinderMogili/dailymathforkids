    - EN: Using the Binomial Theorem, what is the coefficient of x² in the expansion of (x + 3)⁴?
    - FR: En utilisant le théorème binomial, quel est le coefficient de x² dans le développement de (x + 3)⁴?
    - Choices: A) 36  B) 54  C) 72  D) 108
    - Hint: In (a + b)ⁿ, the term with xᵏ uses C(n, k) × aᵏ × bⁿ⁻ᵏ. For (x + 2)³, the x¹ term is C(3,1) × x¹ × 2² = 3 × 4 × x = 12x.
    - Steps:
      - The x² term in (x + 3)⁴: use C(4, 2) × x² × 3².
      - C(4, 2) = 6. 3² = 9. Coefficient = 6 × 9 = 54.
    - Answer: 54
