    • Steps:
      • Start at 7 and count on 3 more.
      • 7… 8, 9, 10 — that is 10 mittens.
    • Answer: 10

      • a⃗ + b⃗ = (1, 3).
    • Answer: (1, 3)

  7. [Medium] Probability Distributions

    • EN: A fair coin is flipped 3 times. What is the probability of getting exactly 2 heads?
    • FR: On lance une pièce de monnaie équilibrée 3 fois. Quelle est la probabilité d'obtenir exactement 2 faces?
    • Choices: A) 1/8 B) 2/8 C) 3/8 D) 4/8
    • Hint: Use C(n, k) × pᵏ × (1−p)ⁿ⁻ᵏ. For 2 heads in 3 flips: C(3,2) × (0.5)² × (0.5)¹ = 3 × 0.25 × 0.5.
    • Steps:
      • C(3, 2) = 3. Each outcome has probability (0.5)³ = 1/8.
      • P = C(3,2) × (1/2)² × (1/2)¹ = 3 × 1/4 × 1/2 = 3/8.
      • The probability is 3/8.
    • Answer: 3/8

  8. [Medium] Derivative — Rate of Change

    • EN: A ball's position is given by s(t) = 4t² − 3t + 1. What is the instantaneous velocity (derivative) at t = 2?
    • FR: La position d'une balle est donnée par s(t) = 4t² − 3t + 1. Quelle est la vitesse instantanée (dérivée) à t = 2?
    • Choices: A) 9 B) 11 C) 13 D) 16
    • Hint: First find s'(t) using the power rule, then substitute the given t-value. For s(t) = 3t² + 2, s'(t) = 6t; at t = 3, s'(3) = 18.
    • Steps:
      • s'(t) = 8t − 3.
      • At t = 2: s'(2) = 8(2) − 3 = 16 − 3 = 13.
      • The instantaneous velocity is 13.
    • Answer: 13

  9. [Hard] Binomial Theorem

    • EN: Using the binomial theorem, what is the coefficient of x² in the expansion of (x + 3)⁴?
    • FR: En utilisant le théorème binomial, quel est le coefficient de x² dans le développement de (x + 3)⁴?
    • Choices: A) 18 B) 54 C) 81 D) 108
    • Hint: The term containing xᵏ in (x + a)ⁿ is C(n, k) × xᵏ × aⁿ⁻ᵏ. For x² in (x + 2)⁴: C(4,2) × x² × 2² = 6 × 4 × x² = 24x².
    • Steps:
      • The x² term: C(4, 2) × x² × 3⁴⁻².
      • C(4, 2) = 6 and 3² = 9.
      • Coefficient = 6 × 9 = 54.
    • Answer: 54

  10. [Hard] Derivatives — Optimization

    • EN: A rectangular field is to be enclosed with 60 m of fencing on three sides (the fourth side is a wall). What width w maximizes the area, and what is that maximum area?
    • FR: Un champ rectangulaire doit être entouré de 60 m de clôture sur trois côtés (le quatrième côté est un mur). Quelle largeur w maximise l'aire, et quelle est cette aire maximale?
    • Choices: A) w = 15 m, Area = 450 m² B) w = 20 m, Area = 400 m² C) w = 15 m, Area = 225 m² D) w = 30 m, Area = 450 m²
    • Hint: With two widths (w) and one length (l): 2w + l = total fencing. Express l in terms of w, write Area = l × w, then take the derivative and set it equal to zero.
    • Steps:
      • Constraint: 2w + l = 60 → l = 60 − 2w.
      • Area A = w × l = w(60 − 2w) = 60w − 2w².
      • A'(w) = 60 − 4w = 0 → w = 15 m; A = 15 × (60 − 30) = 15 × 30 = 450 m².
    • Answer: w = 15 m, Area = 450 m²
