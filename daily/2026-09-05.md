     - Set f′(x) = 0: 2x − 6 = 0 → x = 3.
     - Since the leading coefficient is positive, this is a minimum.
   - Answer: x = 3

9. **[Hard] Binomial Theorem**
   - EN: What is the coefficient of x² in the expansion of (x + 3)⁴?
   - FR: Quel est le coefficient de x² dans le développement de (x + 3)⁴?
   - Choices: A) 54  B) 81  C) 108  D) 216
   - Hint: Use the binomial theorem: the term with x^k is C(n, k) × x^k × 3^(n−k). For example, in (x + 2)³, the x¹ term is C(3,1) × x × 2² = 3 × 4 × x = 12x.
   - Steps:
     - Term with x²: C(4, 2) × x² × 3^(4−2).
     - C(4, 2) = 6; 3² = 9.
     - Coefficient = 6 × 9 = 54.
   - Answer: 54

10. **[Hard] Derivative — Application (Rate of Change)**
    - EN: The position of a particle (in metres) is given by s(t) = t³ − 6t² + 9t, where t is in seconds. What is the velocity of the particle at t = 4 s?
    - FR: La position d'une particule (en mètres) est donnée par s(t) = t³ − 6t² + 9t, où t est en secondes. Quelle est la vitesse de la particule à t = 4 s?
    - Choices: A) 4 m/s  B) 7 m/s  C) 9 m/s  D) 16 m/s
    - Hint: Velocity = derivative of position. Find v(t) = s′(t), then evaluate at the given time. For example, s(t) = t² − 4t: v(t) = 2t − 4; at t = 3, v = 2.
    - Steps:
      - v(t) = s′(t) = 3t² − 12t + 9.
      - At t = 4: v(4) = 3(16) − 12(4) + 9 = 48 − 48 + 9.
      - v(4) = 9 m/s.
    - Answer: 9 m/s

## Mini Story of Kindness
