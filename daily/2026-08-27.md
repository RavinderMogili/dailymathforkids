Priya lived with her grandparents in Sudbury, Ontario. One Saturday, she noticed her Nani struggling to sort through old boxes in the attic. Priya spent the whole afternoon helping label the boxes and carry them to the right rooms. When they finished, Nani smiled and said, "You have made this place feel like home again."
