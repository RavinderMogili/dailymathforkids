    - EN: A farmer has 80 m of fencing to enclose a rectangular field along a river (no fence needed on one side). What width x maximizes the area?
    - FR: Un fermier a 80 m de clôture pour entourer un champ rectangulaire le long d'une rivière (aucune clôture du côté de la rivière). Quelle largeur x maximise l'aire?
    - Choices: A) x = 10 m  B) x = 20 m  C) x = 30 m  D) x = 40 m
    - Hint: Set up the constraint, write Area as a function of one variable, then find A'(x) = 0 — like P = 2x + y = 60, A = x(60 − 2x).
    - Steps:
      - Fence constraint: 2x + L = 80, so L = 80 − 2x.
      - Area A = x(80 − 2x) = 80x − 2x².
      - A'(x) = 80 − 4x = 0 → x = 20. Width = 20 m.
    - Answer: x = 20 m

10. **[Hard] Binomial Theorem**
    - EN: Using the Binomial Theorem, find the coefficient of x² in the expansion of (x + 3)⁴.
    - FR: En utilisant le théorème binomial, trouve le coefficient de x² dans le développement de (x + 3)⁴.
    - Choices: A) 27  B) 54  C) 81  D) 108
    - Hint: The r-th term in (a + b)ⁿ is C(n, r) × aⁿ⁻ʳ × bʳ — like the x² term in (x + 2)⁴: C(4,2) × x² × 2² = 6 × 4x² = 24x².
    - Steps:
      - We need x²: so n − r = 2 → r = 2 in (x + 3)⁴.
      - Term = C(4, 2) × x² × 3² = 6 × x² × 9 = 54x².
      - The coefficient of x² is 54.
    - Answer: 54
