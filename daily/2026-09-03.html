    • EN: There are 7 apples on the table. Emma puts 5 more apples on the table. How many apples are there in all?
    • FR: Il y a 7 pommes sur la table. Emma met 5 autres pommes sur la table. Combien y a-t-il de pommes en tout?

      • x² ÷ x = x. Multiply: x(x + 1) = x² + x. Subtract: (x² − x) − (x² + x) = −2x. Bring down −2: −2x − 2. −2x ÷ x = −2. Check: −2(x + 1) = −2x − 2. Remainder = 0. Quotient = 2x² + x − 2.
    • Answer: 2x² + x − 2
