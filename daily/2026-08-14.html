Fatima was riding the school bus home in Thunder Bay when she noticed an older student, Mr. Chen, struggling to carry a heavy box of library books to the back. Without being asked, Fatima jumped up and held the box steady while he shuffled into his seat. "Thank you so much," Mr. Chen said, his eyes crinkling with a warm smile. Fatima just grinned and thought that small acts of help can make a big difference on a cold Canadian afternoon.
