   - Hint: Find s'(t) using the power rule, then substitute the value of t. For p(t) = 2t² + t, p'(t) = 4t + 1, so at t = 3: p'(3) = 13 m/s.
   - Steps:
     - s'(t) = 6t + 2 (using the power rule).
     - Substitute t = 4: s'(4) = 6(4) + 2 = 24 + 2.
     - s'(4) = 26 m/s.
   - Answer: 26 m/s

10. **[Hard] Normal Distribution — Z-score**
    - EN: Test scores are normally distributed with a mean of 75 and a standard deviation of 10. What is the z-score for a student who scored 95?
    - FR: Les résultats d'un test suivent une distribution normale avec une moyenne de 75 et un écart-type de 10. Quel est le score z d'un élève qui a obtenu 95?
    - Choices: A) 1.5  B) 1.8  C) 2.0  D) 2.5
    - Hint: z = (x − μ) ÷ σ. For x = 85, mean = 70, sd = 10: z = (85 − 70) ÷ 10 = 1.5.
    - Steps:
      - z = (x − μ) ÷ σ.
      - z = (95 − 75) ÷ 10 = 20 ÷ 10.
      - z = 2.0.
    - Answer: 2.0
