     - (x + 2)³ = x³ + 3x²(2) + 3x(2²) + 2³.
     - = x³ + 6x² + 12x + 8.
     - The coefficient of x² is 6.
   - Answer: 6

10. **[Hard] Vectors — Dot Product**
    - EN: Given vectors a = (4, 3) and b = (2, −5), what is the dot product a · b?
    - FR: Étant donné les vecteurs a = (4, 3) et b = (2, −5), combien fait le produit scalaire a · b?
    - Choices: A) −7  B) −8  C) 7  D) 8
    - Hint: Dot product = (a₁ × b₁) + (a₂ × b₂). For example, (3, 2) · (4, −1) = 12 + (−2) = 10.
    - Steps:
