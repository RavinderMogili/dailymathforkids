Grade 1 Problems

  1. [Easy] Counting Apples

    • EN: There are 7 apples on the table. Liam puts 5 more apples on the table. How many apples are there altogether?
    • FR: Il y a 7 pommes sur la table. Liam y met 5 autres pommes. Combien y a-t-il de pommes en tout?
    • Choices: A) 10 B) 11 C) 12 D) 13
    • Hint: To add two numbers, count on from the bigger number (e.g., 6 + 3: start at 6, count 3 more → 7, 8, 9).
    • Steps:
      • Start at 7.
      • Count on 5 more: 8, 9, 10, 11, 12.
      • So the total is 12.
    • Answer: 12

    • EN: The position of a hockey puck is given by s(t) = 3t² − 8t + 5 (in metres, t in seconds). What is the instantaneous velocity at t = 4 seconds?
    • FR: La position d'une rondelle de hockey est donnée par s(t) = 3t² − 8t + 5 (en mètres, t en secondes). Quelle est la vitesse instantanée à t = 4 secondes?
    • Choices: A) 12 m/s B) 16 m/s C) 20 m/s D) 24 m/s
    • Hint: Find v(t) = s'(t) using the power rule, then substitute t (e.g., s(t) = 2t² − 6t: s'(t) = 4t − 6; at t = 3: v = 12 − 6 = 6 m/s).
    • Steps:
      • s'(t) = 6t − 8.
      • At t = 4: v = 6(4) − 8.
      • v = 24 − 8 = 16 m/s.
    • Answer: 16 m/s

  10. [Hard] Binomial Theorem

    • EN: Find the coefficient of the x² term in the expansion of (x + 3)⁴.
    • FR: Trouve le coefficient du terme en x² dans le développement de (x + 3)⁴.
    • Choices: A) 27 B) 54 C) 81 D) 108
    • Hint: Use the binomial theorem: the term with xᵏ is C(n, k) × xᵏ × (constant)ⁿ⁻ᵏ (e.g., in (x + 2)³, the x term: C(3,1) × x × 2² = 3 × 4x = 12x, coefficient = 12).
    • Steps:
      • We need the x² term in (x + 3)⁴; this means k = 2.
      • Term = C(4, 2) × x² × 3² = 6 × x² × 9.
      • Coefficient = 6 × 9 = 54.
    • Answer: 54
