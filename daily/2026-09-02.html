Grade 1 Problems

  1. [Easy] Counting to 20

    • EN: There are 7 apples on the table and 5 more apples in a basket. How many apples are there in all?
    • FR: Il y a 7 pommes sur la table et 5 autres pommes dans un panier. Combien y a-t-il de pommes en tout?
    • Choices: A) 10 B) 11 C) 12 D) 13
    • Hint: To add, count on from the bigger number — 6 + 3 means start at 6 and count 3 more.
    • Steps:
      • Start at 7 and count on 5 more: 8, 9, 10, 11, 12.
      • So there are 12 apples in all.
    • Answer: 12

  2. [Easy] Skip Counting by 2s

    • EN: Skip count by 2s. What comes next? 2, 4, 6, 8, ___
    • FR: Compte par bonds de 2. Quel est le prochain nombre? 2, 4, 6, 8, ___
    • Choices: A) 9 B) 10 C) 11 D) 12
    • Hint: Skip counting by 2s means you add 2 each time — try 12, 14, 16 to see the pattern.
    • Steps:
      • Each number is 2 more than the last.
      • 8 + 2 = 10.
    • Answer: 10

  3. [Easy] Comparing Numbers

    • EN: Which number is greater: 14 or 9?
    • FR: Quel nombre est le plus grand : 14 ou 9?
    • Choices: A) 9 B) 14 C) Both are equal D) Neither
    • Hint: Compare the tens digit first — a number with a tens digit is always bigger than a one-digit number.
    • Steps:
      • 14 has a 1 in the tens place; 9 has no tens.
      • So 14 is greater than 9.
    • Answer: 14

  4. [Easy] Coin Recognition

    • EN: You have 1 nickel. How many cents is that worth?
    • FR: Tu as 1 pièce de 5 cents. Combien de cents vaut-elle?
    • Choices: A) 1 cent B) 5 cents C) 10 cents D) 25 cents
    • Hint: A dime is worth 10 cents — a nickel is worth half of a dime.
    • Steps:
      • A nickel is the coin worth 5 cents in Canada.
      • So 1 nickel = 5 cents.
    • Answer: 5 cents

  5. [Medium] Subtraction within 20

    • EN: There were 15 hockey cards on the table. A friend took 6. How many cards are left?

    • Answer: 9

  6. [Medium] Skip Counting by 5s

  8. [Medium] Counting to 100

    • FR: Quel nombre vient juste avant 100?

  9. [Hard] Adding Three Numbers

    • EN: Sofia has 4 stickers, then gets 5 more, and then gets 3 more. How many stickers does she have in all?
    • FR: Sofia a 4 autocollants, puis en reçoit 5 de plus, puis 3 de plus. Combien d'autocollants a-t-elle en tout?

Grade 2 Problems

    • EN: What is 34 + 25?
    • FR: Combien fait 34 + 25?
    • Choices: A) 57 B) 58 C) 59 D) 61
    • Hint: Add the ones first, then the tens — 12 + 13: ones 2 + 3 = 5, tens 1 + 1 = 2, answer 25.
    • Steps:
      • Ones: 4 + 5 = 9.
      • Tens: 3 + 2 = 5. So 34 + 25 = 59.
    • Answer: 59

  2. [Easy] Telling Time — Hour

    • EN: The clock shows the short hand on 3 and the long hand on 12. What time is it?
    • FR: L'aiguille courte est sur le 3 et la grande aiguille est sur le 12. Quelle heure est-il?
    • Choices: A) 12:03 B) 3:00 C) 3:30 D) 12:15
    • Hint: When the long hand points to 12, it is exactly on the hour — 7:00, for example.
    • Steps:
      • Long hand on 12 means it is exactly on the hour.
      • Short hand on 3 means it is 3 o'clock: 3:00.
    • Answer: 3:00

  3. [Easy] Measuring Length

    • EN: A pencil is 14 cm long and an eraser is 3 cm long. How much longer is the pencil than the eraser?
    • FR: Un crayon mesure 14 cm de long et une gomme mesure 3 cm. De combien le crayon est-il plus long que la gomme?
    • Choices: A) 9 cm B) 10 cm C) 11 cm D) 17 cm
    • Hint: "How much longer" means subtract — if a ruler is 20 cm and a pen is 8 cm, the ruler is 12 cm longer.
    • Steps:
      • Subtract: 14 − 3 = 11.
      • The pencil is 11 cm longer than the eraser.
    • Answer: 11 cm

  4. [Easy] Counting Coins

    • FR: Tu as 2 pièces de 10 cents et 1 pièce de 5 cents. Combien d'argent as-tu?

      • 2 dimes = 20 cents, 1 nickel = 5 cents.

  5. [Medium] Subtraction within 100

    • EN: There are 82 students at a school. 47 of them go outside for recess. How many stay inside?
    • FR: Il y a 82 élèves à l'école. 47 d'entre eux sortent pour la récréation. Combien restent à l'intérieur?
    • Choices: A) 33 B) 35 C) 37 D) 45
    • Hint: To subtract a 2-digit number, subtract the ones then the tens — 56 − 23: ones 6 − 3 = 3, tens 5 − 2 = 3, answer 33.
    • Steps:
      • Ones: 12 − 7 = 5 (regroup: borrow 1 ten from 8).
      • Tens: 7 − 4 = 3. So 82 − 47 = 35.
    • Answer: 35

  6. [Medium] Telling Time — Half Hour

    • EN: The clock shows the short hand between 6 and 7, and the long hand on 6. What time is it?

    • Hint: When the long hand points to 6, it is always half past the hour — for example, 2:30.
    • Steps:
      • Long hand on 6 means 30 minutes past the hour.
      • Short hand between 6 and 7 means the hour is 6: so it is 6:30.
    • Answer: 6:30

  9. [Hard] Two-Step Addition and Subtraction

    • EN: Maya had 60 stickers. She gave 18 to her friend and then got 9 more from her teacher. How many stickers does she have now?
    • FR: Maya avait 60 autocollants. Elle en a donné 18 à son ami, puis en a reçu 9 de son professeur. Combien d'autocollants a-t-elle maintenant?

  10. [Hard] Measuring and Comparing

    • EN: A red ribbon is 45 cm long. A blue ribbon is 28 cm long. How long are both ribbons together?

Grade 3 Problems

  1. [Easy] Multiplication by 2

    • EN: What is 2 × 8?
    • FR: Combien fait 2 × 8?
    • Choices: A) 10 B) 14 C) 16 D) 18
    • Hint: Multiply by 2 means double the number — 2 × 6 = 12.
    • Steps:

      • 8 + 8 = 16.
    • Answer: 16

  2. [Easy] Multiplication by 5

    • EN: What is 5 × 6?
    • FR: Combien fait 5 × 6?
    • Choices: A) 25 B) 30 C) 35 D) 36
    • Hint: Multiplying by 5 always ends in 0 or 5 — 5 × 4 = 20.
    • Steps:
      • 5 × 6 means five groups of 6, or count by 5s six times.
      • 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30.
    • Answer: 30

  3. [Easy] Basic Fractions

    • FR: Une pizza est coupée en 4 parts égales. Emma mange 1 part. Quelle fraction de la pizza a-t-elle mangée?
    • Choices: A) 1/2 B) 1/3 C) 1/4 D) 2/4
    • Hint: A fraction names part of a whole — if something is cut into 3 equal pieces and you take 1, that is 1/3.

      • The pizza is cut into 4 equal pieces (denominator = 4).
      • Emma ate 1 piece, so the fraction is 1/4.

  6. [Medium] Basic Division

  8. [Medium] Fractions — Halves and Quarters

    • FR: Quelle fraction est la plus grande : 1/2 ou 1/4?

Grade 4 Problems

  1. [Easy] Multiplication to 9×9

    • EN: What is 7 × 8?
    • FR: Combien fait 7 × 8?
    • Choices: A) 54 B) 56 C) 63 D) 64
    • Hint: Break it apart — 7 × 8 is like 7 × 4 × 2; for example, 6 × 7 = 6 × 5 + 6 × 2 = 42.
    • Steps:
      • 7 × 8: think of 7 × 4 = 28, then double it.
      • 28 × 2 = 56.
    • Answer: 56

  2. [Easy] Decimal Place Value

    • EN: What is the value of the digit 3 in the number 4.37?
    • FR: Quelle est la valeur du chiffre 3 dans le nombre 4,37?
    • Choices: A) 3 ones B) 3 tenths C) 3 hundredths D) 30
    • Hint: In a decimal, the first digit after the dot is tenths — in 5.72, the 7 is in the tenths place.
    • Steps:
      • In 4.37: 4 is ones, 3 is tenths, 7 is hundredths.
      • The digit 3 is in the tenths place, so its value is 3 tenths.
    • Answer: 3 tenths

  3. [Easy] Area of a Rectangle

    • EN: A rectangular garden is 6 m long and 4 m wide. What is its area?
    • FR: Un jardin rectangulaire a 6 m de long et 4 m de large. Quelle est son aire?
    • Choices: A) 10 m² B) 20 m² C) 24 m² D) 28 m²
    • Hint: Area of a rectangle = length × width — a 5 m by 3 m room has area 5 × 3 = 15 m².
    • Steps:
      • Area = length × width = 6 × 4.
      • 6 × 4 = 24 m².
    • Answer: 24 m²

  4. [Easy] Comparing Fractions

    • EN: Which fraction is greater: 3/4 or 2/4?
    • FR: Quelle fraction est la plus grande : 3/4 ou 2/4?
    • Choices: A) 2/4 B) 3/4 C) They are equal D) Cannot be determined
    • Hint: When the denominators are the same, the bigger numerator wins — 5/8 > 3/8.
    • Steps:
      • Both fractions have denominator 4, so compare numerators.
      • 3 > 2, so 3/4 > 2/4.
    • Answer: 3/4

  5. [Medium] Long Division with Remainders

    • EN: What is 57 ÷ 6?
    • FR: Combien fait 57 ÷ 6?
    • Choices: A) 8 remainder 9 B) 9 remainder 3 C) 9 remainder 6 D) 10 remainder 3
    • Hint: Find how many times the divisor fits into the dividend — 25 ÷ 4: 4 × 6 = 24, so the answer is 6 remainder 1.
    • Steps:
      • 6 × 9 = 54, which is the largest multiple of 6 that fits in 57.
      • 57 − 54 = 3, so 57 ÷ 6 = 9 remainder 3.
    • Answer: 9 remainder 3

  6. [Medium] Elapsed Time

    • Steps:

  7. [Medium] Decimal Operations

  8. [Medium] Multiplication — Two-Digit

  9. [Hard] Multi-Step Area and Perimeter

    • EN: A rectangular hockey rink is 9 m long and 5 m wide. What is the difference between its area and its perimeter?
    • FR: Une patinoire rectangulaire a 9 m de long et 5 m de large. Quelle est la différence entre son aire et son périmètre?
    • Choices: A) 17 m B) 17 m² C) 21 m² D) 45 m²
