   - Hint: Use synthetic division or long division step by step. For (x³ + 3x² − 4x) ÷ (x − 1): test x = 1 in the polynomial and proceed step by step.
   - Steps:
     - Synthetic division with root x = 2: bring down 1; 1×2=2, 2+2=4; 4×2=8, −5+8=3; 3×2=6, −6+6=0.
     - Coefficients of quotient: 1, 4, 3 → x² + 4x + 3.
     - The quotient is x² + 4x + 3.
   - Answer: x² + 4x + 3

9. **[Hard] Probability — Conditional Probability**
   - EN: In a Grade 12 class, 60% of students play a sport and 30% of students play a sport and are on the honour roll. Given that a student plays a sport, what is the probability they are also on the honour roll?
   - FR: Dans une classe de 12e année, 60 % des élèves pratiquent un sport et 30 % des élèves pratiquent un sport et sont au tableau d'honneur. Sachant qu'un élève pratique un sport, quelle est la probabilité qu'il soit aussi au tableau d'honneur?
   - Choices: A) 50%  B) 30%  C) 40%  D) 60%
   - Hint: P(B|A) = P(A and B) / P(A). For P(A) = 0.40 and P(A and B) = 0.10: P(B|A) = 0.10/0.40 = 25%.
   - Steps:
     - P(sport) = 0.60; P(sport and honour roll) = 0.30.
     - P(honour roll | sport) = 0.30 / 0.60.
     - = 0.50 = 50%.
   - Answer: 50%

10. **[Hard] Vector — Dot Product and Angle**
    - EN: Find the angle between vectors **a** = (3, 4) and **b** = (4, 3). Use cos θ = (**a** · **b**) / (|**a**| |**b**|). Round to the nearest degree. (cos⁻¹(0.96) ≈ 16°)
    - FR: Trouve l'angle entre les vecteurs **a** = (3, 4) et **b** = (4, 3). Utilise cos θ = (**a** · **b**) / (|**a**| |**b**|). Arrondis au degré près. (cos⁻¹(0,96) ≈ 16°)
    - Choices: A) 45°  B) 30°  C) 16°  D) 20°
    - Hint: Dot product = a₁b₁ + a₂b₂. Both magnitudes = √(a₁² + a₂²). Then divide and find the inverse cosine.
    - Steps:
      - **a** · **b** = 3×4 + 4×3 = 12 + 12 = 24.
      - |**a**| = √(9+16) = 5; |**b**| = √(16+9) = 5.
      - cos θ = 24/25 = 0.96; θ = cos⁻¹(0.96) ≈ 16°.
    - Answer: 16°
