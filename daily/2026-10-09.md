   - FR: Le vecteur **a** = (3, –1) et le vecteur **b** = (–2, 4). Quel est **a** + **b**?
   - Choices: A) (1, 3)  B) (5, 3)  C) (1, 5)  D) (–1, 3)
   - Hint: Add corresponding components — like (4, 2) + (–1, 5) = (4+(–1), 2+5) = (3, 7).
   - Steps:
     - x-component: 3 + (–2) = 1.
     - y-component: –1 + 4 = 3.
     - **a** + **b** = (1, 3).
   - Answer: (1, 3)

8. **[Medium] Polynomial Division**
   - EN: Divide (x³ – 2x² + x – 3) by (x – 1) using synthetic or long division. What is the remainder?
   - FR: Divise (x³ – 2x² + x – 3) par (x – 1) en utilisant la division synthétique ou longue. Quel est le reste?
   - Choices: A) –1  B) –3  C) 0  D) 3
   - Hint: Use the Remainder Theorem: the remainder when dividing by (x–a) is f(a) — like f(2) gives the remainder when dividing by (x–2).
   - Steps:
     - f(1) = 1³ – 2(1)² + 1 – 3 = 1 – 2 + 1 – 3 = –3.
     - Remainder = –3.
   - Answer: –3

9. **[Hard] Binomial Probability**
   - EN: A student guesses on 5 true/false questions. What is the probability of getting exactly 3 correct? (Each guess is correct with probability 0.5.)
   - FR: Un élève devine sur 5 questions vrai/faux. Quelle est la probabilité d'en avoir exactement 3 correctes? (Chaque supposition est correcte avec une probabilité de 0,5.)
   - Choices: A) 0.25  B) 0.3125  C) 0.375  D) 0.5
   - Hint: P(X=k) = C(n,k) × pᵏ × (1–p)ⁿ⁻ᵏ — like n=4, k=2, p=0.5: C(4,2)×0.25×0.25 = 6×0.0625 = 0.375.
   - Steps:
     - P(X=3) = C(5,3) × (0.5)³ × (0.5)².
     - C(5,3) = 10.
     - = 10 × 0.125 × 0.25 = 10 × 0.03125 = 0.3125.
   - Answer: 0.3125

10. **[Hard] Derivative — Optimization**
    - EN: A farmer wants to fence a rectangular field along a river (no fence on the river side). He has 120 m of fencing for the three other sides. What width maximizes the area of the field?
    - FR: Un agriculteur veut clôturer un champ rectangulaire le long d'une rivière (pas de clôture du côté de la rivière). Il a 120 m de clôture pour les trois autres côtés. Quelle largeur maximise l'aire du champ?
    - Choices: A) 20 m  B) 30 m  C) 40 m  D) 60 m
    - Hint: Let width = w (two sides) and length = l; then 2w + l = total fencing, so l = total – 2w; area = w×l; find derivative and set to zero — like 80 m fencing: 2w+l=80, A=w(80–2w)=80w–2w², A'=80–4w=0, w=20.
    - Steps:
      - Let width = w; length = 120 – 2w.
      - Area A(w) = w(120 – 2w) = 120w – 2w².
      - A'(w) = 120 – 4w = 0.
      - 4w = 120, w = 30 m.
    - Answer: 30 m
