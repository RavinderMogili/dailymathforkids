Grade 1 Problems

  1. [Easy] Counting to 20

    • EN: There are 7 apples in a bowl. Emma adds 5 more apples. How many apples are there now?
    • FR: Il y a 7 pommes dans un bol. Emma en ajoute 5 autres. Combien y a-t-il de pommes maintenant?
    • Choices: A) 10 B) 11 C) 12 D) 13
    • Hint: Try counting on from the bigger number. Start at 8, count 4 more: 9, 10, 11, 12.
    • Steps:
      • Start at 7.
      • Count on 5 more: 8, 9, 10, 11, 12.
      • So there are 12 apples in total.
    • Answer: 12

  2. [Easy] Skip Counting by 2s

    • EN: Skip count by 2s. What number comes next: 4, 6, 8, ___?
    • FR: Compte par bonds de 2. Quel nombre vient ensuite: 4, 6, 8, ___?
    • Choices: A) 9 B) 10 C) 11 D) 12
    • Hint: When skip counting by 2s, add 2 each time. For example: 10, 12, 14 — add 2 each step.
    • Steps:
      • The pattern adds 2 each time.
      • 4 + 2 = 6, 6 + 2 = 8, 8 + 2 = 10.
      • The next number is 10.
    • Answer: 10

    • Steps:
      • 14 has 1 ten and 4 ones.
      • 9 has 0 tens and 9 ones.
      • A number with more tens is bigger, so 14 is bigger.
    • Answer: 14
