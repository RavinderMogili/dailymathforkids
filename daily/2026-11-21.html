  10. [Hard] Optimization with Derivatives

    • EN: A rectangular garden has a fixed perimeter of 20 meters. What is the maximum area?
    • FR: Un jardin rectangulaire a un périmètre fixe de 20 mètres. Quelle est la surface maximale?
    • Choices: A) 10 B) 20 C) 25 D) 50
    • Hint: Express area as a function of one variable and find its maximum.
    • Steps:
      • Step 1: Let length = x, width = (20 - 2x)/2 = 10 - x.
      • Step 2: Area = x(10 - x) = 10x - x².
      • Step 3: Find derivative: dA/dx = 10 - 2x. Set to 0: 10 - 2x = 0 → x = 5.
      • Step 4: Area = 5(10 - 5) = 25.
    • Answer: 25
