Grade 1 Problems

  1. [Easy] Counting Apples

    • EN: Mia has 7 apples. Her friend gives her 5 more. How many apples does Mia have now?
    • FR: Mia a 7 pommes. Son amie lui en donne 5 de plus. Combien de pommes Mia a-t-elle maintenant?
    • Choices: A) 10 apples B) 11 apples C) 12 apples D) 13 apples
    • Hint: Count on from the bigger number — if you had 6 and got 4 more, count 7, 8, 9, 10.
    • Steps:
      • Start at 7 and count on 5 more.
      • 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 — that is 5 hops forward.
      • So Mia has 12 apples.
    • Answer: 12 apples

  2. [Easy] Coin Recognition

    • EN: Which Canadian coin is worth 10 cents?
    • FR: Quelle pièce canadienne vaut 10 cents?
    • Choices: A) A penny B) A nickel C) A dime D) A quarter
    • Hint: Think about which coin is small and silver — a penny is copper and worth 1 cent.
    • Steps:
      • A penny = 1 cent.
      • A nickel = 5 cents.
      • A dime = 10 cents.
      • The answer is a dime.
    • Answer: A dime

  3. [Easy] Skip Counting by 5s

    • EN: Count by 5s. What number comes after 15?
    • FR: Compte par bonds de 5. Quel nombre vient après 15?
    • Choices: A) 16 B) 18 C) 20 D) 25
    • Hint: When skip counting by 5s, always add 5 to the last number — 5, 10, 15, then add 5 again.
    • Steps:
      • The pattern is 5, 10, 15, ...
      • Add 5 to 15: 15 + 5 = 20.
      • The next number is 20.
    • Answer: 20

  4. [Easy] Comparing Numbers

    • FR: Quel nombre est plus grand : 14 ou 9?

    • Steps:

      • 9 has 0 tens and 9 ones.
      • 14 is greater than 9.
    • Answer: 14

  5. [Medium] Subtraction Within 100
