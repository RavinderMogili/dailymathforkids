    • Steps:

    • FR: Combien font 20 ÷ 4?

    • Steps:

      • 4 × 5 = 20.
      • So 20 ÷ 4 = 5.

  1. [Easy] Multiplication Table

    • EN: What is 7 × 8?
    • FR: Combien font 7 × 8?
    • Choices: A) 54 B) 56 C) 63 D) 64
    • Hint: Try breaking it apart. 7 × 8 = 7 × 4 + 7 × 4. Use a fact you already know.
    • Steps:
      • 7 × 8 = 7 × (4 + 4).
      • 7 × 4 = 28, so 28 + 28 = 56.
      • So 7 × 8 = 56.
    • Answer: 56

  2. [Easy] Decimal Place Value

    • EN: What is the value of the digit 4 in the number 3.47?
    • FR: Quelle est la valeur du chiffre 4 dans le nombre 3,47?
    • Choices: A) 4 ones B) 4 tenths C) 4 hundredths D) 40 ones
    • Hint: After the decimal point, the first place is tenths, the second place is hundredths.
    • Steps:
      • In 3.47, the digit 3 is in the ones place.
      • The digit 4 is in the first place after the decimal point.
      • That is the tenths place, so 4 tenths.
    • Answer: 4 tenths
