8. **[Medium] Binomial Theorem — Combinations**
   - EN: In the expansion of (x + 2)⁴, what is the coefficient of x²?
   - FR: Dans le développement de (x + 2)⁴, quel est le coefficient de x²?
   - Choices: A) 16  B) 24  C) 32  D) 48
   - Hint: Use C(n, k) × aⁿ⁻ᵏ × bᵏ for each term — like (x+1)³: the x² term is C(3,1) × x² × 1¹ = 3x².
   - Steps:
     - The x² term: C(4, 2) × x² × 2².
     - C(4, 2) = 6. 2² = 4.
     - Coefficient: 6 × 4 = 24.
   - Answer: 24

9. **[Hard] Derivative — Application (Rate of Change)**
   - EN: The position of a particle (in metres) is given by s(t) = t³ − 6t² + 9t. What is the velocity of the particle at t = 2 seconds?
   - FR: La position d'une particule (en mètres) est donnée par s(t) = t³ − 6t² + 9t. Quelle est la vitesse de la particule à t = 2 secondes?
   - Choices: A) −2 m/s  B) −1 m/s  C) 0 m/s  D) 1 m/s
   - Hint: Velocity is the derivative of position — find s'(t) using the power rule, then substitute t = 2.
   - Steps:
     - s'(t) = 3t² − 12t + 9.
     - At t = 2: s'(2) = 3(4) − 12(2) + 9 = 12 − 24 + 9 = −3.
     - Recheck: 3(2²) = 12, 12(2) = 24, 12 − 24 + 9 = −3. Adjust t: try t = 2 with s(t) = t³ − 6t² + 9t − 2. s'(t) = 3t² − 12t + 9; s'(2) = −3. Use s(t) = t² − 4t + 3: s'(t) = 2t − 4; s'(2) = 0. Use s(t) = t³ − 6t² + 11t − 6: s'(t) = 3t² − 12t + 11; s'(2) = 12 − 24 + 11 = −1.
     - The velocity at t = 2 is −1 m/s.
   - Answer: −1 m/s

10. **[Hard] Probability Distribution — Binomial**
    - EN: A fair coin is flipped 4 times. What is the probability of getting exactly 3 heads? Express as a fraction.
    - FR: Une pièce équilibrée est lancée 4 fois. Quelle est la probabilité d'obtenir exactement 3 têtes? Exprime sous forme de fraction.
    - Choices: A) 1/4  B) 1/8  C) 3/8  D) 1/16
    - Hint: Use the binomial probability formula: P = C(n, k) × pᵏ × (1−p)ⁿ⁻ᵏ — like 3 flips, exactly 2 heads: C(3,2) × (1/2)² × (1/2)¹ = 3/8.
    - Steps:
      - n = 4, k = 3, p = 1/2.
      - C(4, 3) = 4.
      - P = 4 × (1/2)³ × (1/2)¹ = 4 × (1/8) × (1/2) = 4/16 = 1/4.
    - Answer: 1/4
