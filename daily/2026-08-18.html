Grade 1 Problems

  1. [Easy] Counting Bears

    • EN: Liam has 7 teddy bears on his shelf. He gets 3 more. How many teddy bears does he have now?
    • FR: Liam a 7 oursons sur son étagère. Il en reçoit 3 de plus. Combien d'oursons a-t-il maintenant?

    • Steps:
      • Start at 7 and count on 3 more.
      • 8, 9, 10 — so Liam has 10 bears.
    • Answer: 10 bears
