    - Choices: A) 400 m²  B) 350 m²  C) 500 m²  D) 450 m²
    - Hint: With constraint 2w + l = 60, write A = w × l, substitute l, differentiate, set to zero (e.g., 2w + l = 40: l = 40−2w, A = w(40−2w), dA/dw = 40−4w = 0, w = 10, A = 200).
    - Steps:
      - Let width = w, then length l = 60 − 2w.
      - Area A = w(60 − 2w) = 60w − 2w².
      - dA/dw = 60 − 4w = 0 → w = 15 m.
      - l = 60 − 2(15) = 30 m. A = 15 × 30 = 450 m².
    - Answer: 450 m²

10. **[Hard] Permutations with Restrictions**
    - EN: How many 3-digit numbers can be formed using the digits 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 if no digit may be repeated and the number must be greater than 300?
    - FR: Combien de nombres à 3 chiffres peut-on former avec les chiffres 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 si aucun chiffre ne peut être répété et que le nombre doit être supérieur à 300?
    - Choices: A) 48  B) 36  C) 24  D) 30
    - Hint: Count by cases based on the first digit — only certain digits can lead (e.g., numbers > 200 using {1,2,3,4}: first digit is 2, 3, or 4, then arrange remaining 3 from the rest).
    - Steps:
      - The hundreds digit must be 3, 4, or 5 (to be greater than 300).
      - For each choice of hundreds digit (3 options), choose 2 digits from the remaining 4: P(4,2) = 4 × 3 = 12.
      - Total: 3 × 12 = 36.
    - Answer: 36
