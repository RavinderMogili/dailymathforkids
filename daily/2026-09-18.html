    • EN: Léa has 7 apples. She picks 5 more. How many apples does she have in all?
    • FR: Léa a 7 pommes. Elle en cueille 5 de plus. Combien de pommes a-t-elle en tout?
    • Choices: A) 10 B) 11 C) 12 D) 13

      • Count on 5 more: 8, 9, 10, 11, 12.
      • So Léa has 12 apples.
    • Answer: 12
